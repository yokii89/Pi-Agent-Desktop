## English

### Mobile remote access (preview)

- Pair a compatible phone by scanning a QR code and confirming the matching verification code on your desktop.
- Choose which projects the phone can access. Grant message sending, task stopping, session creation, and interaction viewing or answering separately.
- Review the granted projects and permissions in Settings, and revoke phone access from your desktop.
- Improve recovery after interrupted remote operations, keep access isolated between projects and devices, and handle redacted interaction choices correctly.

This preview requires a compatible phone app and a trusted relay service. PiDesk must remain running and connected on your desktop.

### Fixes and improvements

- Keep streamed thinking content separate from normal replies, including when thinking tags arrive in multiple pieces.
- Improve extension panel updates under rapid changes and connection interruptions, including Chinese text and emoji split across incoming data.
- Make extension panel opening and delivery failures more reliable to detect, reducing missing or overwritten updates.

## 简体中文

### 手机远程访问（预览）

- 支持使用兼容的手机应用扫描二维码配对，并在电脑端核对一致的验证码。
- 自主选择手机可以访问的项目；发送消息、停止任务、新建会话、查看交互和回答交互均可单独授权。
- 在设置中查看已授权的项目与权限，并随时从电脑端撤销手机访问。
- 改善远程操作中断后的结果核对，隔离不同项目与设备的访问，并正确处理脱敏后的交互选项。

此预览功能需要兼容的手机应用与可信的中转服务，电脑上的 PiDesk 需要保持运行并连接中转。

### 修复与改进

- 正确区分流式输出中的思考内容与普通回复，支持分段到达的思考标签。
- 改善扩展面板在快速更新和连接中断时的表现，修复中文与表情符号分段传输时的显示问题。
- 改善扩展面板打开与更新失败时的处理，减少更新丢失或被旧内容覆盖的问题。
