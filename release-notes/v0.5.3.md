A small patch: the interface can now be zoomed from the keyboard, and the conversation's tool rows line up with the text.

## Zooming the interface

- **Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- resize the whole window.** Press Ctrl+= to zoom in one step, Ctrl+- to zoom out, Ctrl+0 to return to 100%. Each press moves 10%, from 50% up to 200%. Ctrl+= was the combination that did nothing before — the menu entry expected a literal plus key, so zooming in felt broken while zooming out worked.
- **The level is remembered.** Quit at 125% and PiDesk opens at 125% next time, reloads included.

## Fixes & polish

- **Tool rows and the thinking block start where the text starts.** They used to sit a few pixels further right than the paragraphs above and below them, so a long reply looked ragged at the left edge. Rows now share the document's left edge, expanded results included.

## 简体中文

一个小补丁：界面可以用键盘缩放了，会话里的工具行也和正文对齐了。

### 缩放界面

- **Ctrl+= 与 Ctrl+- 调整整个窗口的大小。** Ctrl+= 放大一档，Ctrl+- 缩小一档，Ctrl+0 回到 100%；每档 10%，范围是 50% 到 200%。以前按 Ctrl+= 毫无反应——菜单里那条加速键只认字面的「+」键，于是「能缩小、不能放大」。
- **缩放比例会被记住。** 在 125% 下退出，下次打开还是 125%，重新加载页面也一样。

### 修复与细节

- **工具行和思考块从正文的左缘起笔。** 它们原本比上下段落向右偏几个像素，长回复读起来左边缘是毛的；现在这些行与文档流同一条左缘，展开的结果区跟着对齐。
