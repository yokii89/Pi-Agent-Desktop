## English

### Remote account improvements

- Show your remote account name and avatar in the account panel, with a fallback when profile details or the avatar are unavailable.
- Connect account sign-in to the production relay, improving compatibility with the official remote companion service.
- Keep sign-in available when optional profile details cannot be retrieved.

Remote companion access remains read-only and requires a compatible Pi-Mobile app. PiDesk must remain running and connected to the relay.

## 简体中文

### 远程账号体验改进

- 在账号面板中显示远程账号名称和头像；资料缺失或头像不可用时会显示默认状态。
- 账号登录改为连接正式中转服务，提升与官方远程伴侣服务的兼容性。
- 即使无法获取可选的账号资料，也不会影响正常登录。

手机远程访问仍为只读功能，需要兼容的 Pi-Mobile 应用；电脑上的 PiDesk 需要保持运行并连接中转。
