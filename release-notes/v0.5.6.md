A farewell of its own for the uninstall wizard, plus crisper wizard graphics on high-DPI screens.

## A proper goodbye

- **The uninstall wizard gets its own farewell artwork.** The same light scene as the installer, but with the app quietly fading out — a ghosted app window with a "Thanks for using PiDesk" note — so removing PiDesk feels just as considered as installing it.
- **Sharper wizard graphics.** The sidebar artwork and header banner are now provided at high resolution, so they stay crisp at 100%, 150% and 200% display scaling instead of turning blurry on high-DPI screens.
- **PiDesk's own welcome copy.** The welcome pages of both wizards now carry custom bilingual wording (English or Chinese, following the installer language).

## 简体中文

卸载向导有了自己的告别画面，向导图形在高分屏上也更清晰了。

### 正式的告别

- **卸载向导换上专属告别插画。** 与安装向导同一套浅色场景，但让应用悄然退场——半透明的"幽灵"应用卡配上一行「感谢使用 PiDesk」，卸载 PiDesk 与安装它一样被认真对待。
- **向导图形更锐利。** 侧栏插画与页眉横幅改为高分辨率输出，在 100% / 150% / 200% 显示缩放下都保持清晰，高分屏上不再发糊。
- **PiDesk 自己的欢迎文案。** 安装与卸载向导的欢迎页改用定制的双语措辞（英文 / 简体中文，随安装器语言自动切换）。
