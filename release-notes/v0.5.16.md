This update keeps long messages from taking over the conversation.

## Long messages fold up

- A message longer than four lines now shows just the first few lines, with a soft fade at the bottom and a **Show full message** button.
- Click the message itself to expand it, and click again to fold it back — or use the button.
- Short messages look exactly as before: no fade, no button.

## Nothing important hides

- Images you attached and the startup progress indicator always stay visible, even while the text is folded.
- Copy still copies the entire message, folded or not.
- Selecting text to copy or quote still works; a click that finishes a selection never folds the message by accident.

## 简体中文

本次更新让过长的消息不再占满整个对话。

### 长消息默认收起

- 超过四行的消息只显示前几行，底部带一层柔和渐隐，并提供 **展开全文** 按钮。
- 点击消息本身即可展开，再点一次收回，也可以用按钮切换。
- 短消息保持原样：没有渐隐，也没有按钮。

### 重要的内容不会被藏起来

- 你附上的图片和启动进度始终可见，不会随文字一起被折叠。
- 复制的仍是完整内容，与是否展开无关。
- 划选文字用于复制或引用依旧照常；选中文字松开鼠标时不会顺手把消息折叠掉。
