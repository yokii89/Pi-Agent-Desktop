## English

### Clearer remote sign-in and recovery

- Sign in to your remote account in the system browser, follow the sign-in progress and countdown, and cancel or retry when needed. Reopening the pairing dialog keeps the current sign-in progress.
- See the signed-in account and manage account sign-in separately from the relay connection. Ordinary disconnections now have one clear reconnect action.
- Recover from expired, damaged or unfinished computer registration. Reset a registration, choose an explicitly confirmed local-only reset when offline, or remove old computers from your account directory while keeping projects and conversation history.
- Continue the same registration request after a retry or restart, reducing duplicate computer registrations.
- Receive clearer messages for browser failures, sign-in timeouts, callback port conflicts, network errors, secure storage problems and account mismatches. The browser confirms sign-in only after credentials have been saved securely.

### Consistent pairing experience

- Improve account-card spacing, action-button layout and narrow-window behavior to match PiDesk's settings interface.
- Add Chinese and English sign-in result pages that follow the app's light or dark appearance.
- Improve remote history synchronization efficiency and connection cleanup, and prevent delayed responses from restoring cancelled sign-ins or paused connections.

Remote companion access remains read-only and in preview. This release uses the v2 connection protocol and requires a compatible Pi-Mobile app and a trusted relay with account sign-in enabled. PiDesk must remain running and connected on your desktop.

## 简体中文

### 更清晰的远程登录与恢复

- 在系统浏览器中登录远程账号，查看登录进度和倒计时，并按需取消或重试。关闭后重新打开配对窗口，仍可继续查看当前登录进度。
- 查看当前登录账号，分别管理账号登录与中转连接。普通断线只保留一个明确的重新连接入口。
- 恢复过期、损坏或未完成的电脑登记。可重置登记，在离线时明确确认仅重置本机，或从账号电脑目录中移除旧电脑，同时保留项目和会话历史。
- 重试或重启后继续同一个登记请求，减少重复创建电脑登记。
- 为浏览器打开失败、登录超时、回调端口占用、网络错误、安全存储问题和账号不匹配提供更清楚的提示。只有凭证安全保存成功后，浏览器才会确认账号登录成功。

### 更统一的配对体验

- 改善账号卡片留白、操作按钮布局和窄窗口适配，使其与 PiDesk 设置界面保持一致。
- 新增中英文登录结果页面，跟随应用的明暗外观。
- 提高远程历史同步效率，改善连接清理，避免延迟响应恢复已取消的登录或已暂停的连接。

手机远程访问仍为只读预览功能。本版本使用 v2 连接协议，需要兼容的 Pi-Mobile 应用和已启用账号登录的可信中转服务，电脑上的 PiDesk 需要保持运行并连接中转。
