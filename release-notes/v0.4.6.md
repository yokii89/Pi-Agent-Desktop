An opening of its own, faster cold starts, and thinking levels that follow each model.

## Startup

- **PiDesk has its own opening.** A launch now plays a short sequence — the pi mark, three lines of copy and a light sweep — about four seconds at normal speed, once per launch. It follows your theme: black on dark, white on light.
- **Make it yours.** Settings → Startup animation lets you switch it off, change the speed, replace the logo (an image up to 128 KB) and its size, rewrite the three lines, and pick the font. Every change previews on the spot.
- **Faster cold start.** Checking the pi version no longer holds the window back, and opening a session no longer launches an extra process.

## Models

- **Thinking levels match the model.** The thinking slider in the input bar now offers exactly the levels the current model supports — from Off up to its highest — and lands on the level pi is actually using. In Settings → Models you can set the highest level each model supports; the table shows the level by name ("High", "Minimal") instead of a number.

## Conversation

- **One-line code blocks are compact.** A fenced block holding a single line no longer carries an empty header strip; it renders as one tight row, with copy still at hand.

## 简体中文

自己的开场、更快的冷启动，以及跟着模型走的思考档位。

### 启动

- **PiDesk 有了自己的开场。** 启动时会先播一段约四秒的动画——pi 标识、三行文案和一道扫光，每次启动只播一次。它跟随你的主题：深色档黑底白字，浅色档白底黑字。
- **样式随你改。** 设置 →「启动动画」里可以关掉动画、调整速度、换成自己的标识图片（最大 128 KB）并调整大小、改写三行文案、选择字体，改动即时预览。
- **冷启动更快。** 探测 pi 版本不再挡住窗口出现，打开会话也不再额外拉起进程。

### 模型

- **思考档位与模型对齐。** 输入栏的思考滑杆只列出当前模型真正支持的档位——从「关闭」到它的最高档——并落在 pi 实际使用的档位上。设置 →「模型」里可以为每个模型设定最高支持到哪一档，列表直接显示档名（「高」「极低」），不再显示数字。

### 对话

- **单行代码块更紧凑。** 只有一行代码的围栏不再顶着一条空的标题栏，直接以一行呈现，复制按钮照常可用。
