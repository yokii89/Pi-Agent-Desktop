## English

- Navigate agent graphs more easily with clearer task progress, filtering, and branch focus.
- Preserve queued prompts and attachments when editing or recovering queue actions, and get a clear warning when a message exceeds the supported image limit.
- Use a more consistent search experience across source files, PDF, and Office previews.
- Choose whether to stop or keep background processes in PiDesk's quit dialog.
- Keep image previews stable while files are loading, and reduce excess spacing before subtask notifications.

## 简体中文

- 智能体关系图的任务进度、筛选和分支定位更清晰，浏览任务更方便。
- 编辑或恢复队列操作时保留排队消息与附件；图片数量超出单条消息限制时会明确提示。
- 源码、PDF 和 Office 预览中的搜索体验更加统一。
- 退出应用时可在 PiDesk 对话框中选择停止或保留后台进程。
- 图片加载期间的预览更加稳定，并收紧助手回复与子任务通知之间的多余间距。
